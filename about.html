The Story Behind the Logs

Welcome to my personal space on the web. "Boni's Logs" started as a simple idea: build something fun, quirky, and completely from scratch. No frameworks, no libraries—just pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.


Why Red?

Why not? It’s bold, it’s loud, and it makes debugging feel like a high-stakes emergency. I wanted a design that stands out from the sea of blue and white bootstrap sites.


Tech Stack

  • HTML5 - For semantic structure.
  • CSS3 - Variables, Grid, Flexbox, and Animations.
  • Vanilla JS - DOM manipulation and LocalStorage wizardry.
