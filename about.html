Who I Am

I’m Boni — a developer who enjoys building practical tools from the ground up. I like understanding how things work under the hood, which is why I often avoid heavy frameworks and prefer writing things myself, even if it takes longer.


What I Build

Most of my projects start as personal problems I want to solve. One of them is a Bible desktop app built with Java Swing, focused on fast navigation, bookmarks, and custom user-written books instead of just passive reading. It’s an ongoing experiment in UI logic, structure, and usability.

Another project is a redundant search automator — something born out of frustration with repetitive searches and manual filtering. The goal is simple: automate the boring parts and surface only what actually matters.


Open Source

Most of what I build lives on GitHub. You can explore my projects, experiments, and half-finished ideas here: github.com/Bonsa-Dereje
